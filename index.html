03Look beneath the numbers

Why and How Do People Participate?

Popular activities and demographics are only part of the story. Motivations and organisation add context.

Motivations by age group

Why do people take part, and does it change with age? This compares the reasons people give.

Motivations explain why people take part, not how activity is arranged. Next, we look at who organises it.

Organising entities by age group

Bands show shares of participation, not people moving between groups.

04Move from people to place

From Activity Settings to Geographic Differences

Where activity happens matters. We look at reported settings, then at differences between states and territories.

Where activities take place

Recreation can happen in many settings. This compares how reported settings rank against each other.

Participation by state and territory

State rates do not show individual parks or how often they are visited. So we zoom into the Northern Territory.

05A closer look at one territory

Zooming In: The Northern Territory's Park Landscape

State rates cannot show individual destinations. The NT lets us look at parks run by the NT Government and the Commonwealth.

Why the Northern Territory?

The NT is one of the higher-ranking states and territories and is geographically extensive. Its parks also report visitor numbers. This is a case-study choice, not evidence of better access to parks.

Where NT parks are

Where are the parks, and who manages them? The map separates NT Government sites from Commonwealth-managed Kakadu and Uluru-Kata Tjuta.

Location alone does not show visits. Next, we compare parks with comparable visitor estimates.

Visits to NT Government parks

Which NT Government parks have the highest estimated visits? Symbol size shows reported visitor numbers.

Visitor estimates use the NT Government's reporting period. Only parks with comparable data are shown, so this is not a ranking of every NT park. Kakadu and Uluru-Kata Tjuta are not included.

Park visits and participation measure different things. Next, we return to wider social and economic context.

06Step back for perspective

Sport and recreation tourism output
